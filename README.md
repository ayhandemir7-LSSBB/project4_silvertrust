## How the engagement ran

1. **Client research:** Prepared a 1-page sector analysis as private source material, not shared before the interview.
2. **Discovery interview:** Held a 45-minute interview as consultant, using process questions such as "walk me through last week" instead of "what are your pain points".
3. **Diagnosis:** Found one root cause and confirmed it with the client in a play-back: nobody owns, segments or attributes the bank's marketing and lead data.
4. **Proposal:** Made the deck and brief tell the same story, with every recommendation traced to something heard in the interview.

## Problem and recommendation in one view

**Problem:** "The bank pays for campaigns it cannot target or prove, because nobody owns, segments or attributes its marketing and lead data."

**Recommendation:** Connect the demand-generation journey end to end: data, campaign, capture, sales and results. AI proposes segments, drafts and A/B tests segment campaigns, checks lead data at capture, ranks and routes leads, and links deals to campaigns. People approve segments and content and make every client decision.

**Next step:** Name the data owners and agree two segments to start an 8-week pilot on one campaign.
